Second citizenship

Grenada Citizenship by Investment

A second passport can widen your travel options, protect your family's future and open doors for business. Here is how the Grenada programme works, and how we help.

Why people choose Grenada

  • Citizenship for life. It can be passed on to future generations.
  • Dual citizenship is allowed. You keep your current nationality.
  • No residency requirement. You do not have to live in Grenada to qualify.
  • Wide travel access. Visa-free or visa-on-arrival entry to well over 100 destinations, including the UK, the Schengen area and China. Exact access changes, so we confirm the current list for you.
  • US treaty investor option. Grenada is an E-2 treaty country, which can matter for entrepreneurs. Eligibility depends on your own circumstances and US rules.
  • Family inclusion. Spouses, dependent children and, in some cases, dependent parents can be included.

How you can qualify

The programme offers a government-approved contribution route and an approved real estate route. Amounts, fees and conditions are set by the Grenada government and can change, so we give you the current figures and compare the routes for your family during your consultation rather than quote an out-of-date number here.

How the process works

  1. Consultation. We check your eligibility, family composition and goals.
  2. Route and documents. You choose a route and we prepare your document list.
  3. Application and due diligence. The application is submitted and the government runs background checks on every adult applicant.
  4. Approval and payment. Contributions are paid only after approval in principle.
  5. Citizenship and passport. Certificate issued, then passport application.

Timelines vary. Applicants typically plan for a few months from submission to passport.

How Taste Wayfinders helps

  • Honest eligibility check before you spend money
  • Guidance on route, documents and source-of-funds evidence
  • Application preparation and follow-up
  • Support for the whole family, from first call to passport
Good to know

Every application is decided by the Grenada government after due diligence. No consultant can guarantee approval. Government and due-diligence fees are paid separately from our service fees.
