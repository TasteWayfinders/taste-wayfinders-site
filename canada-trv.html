Canada Visitor Visa (TRV)

What you need to know about Canada's Temporary Resident Visa — fees, how long it's valid, and what to prepare.

Up to 10 yearsMultiple-entry validity
6–12 weeksTypical Nigeria processing
$100 CADApplication fee

A Canada Temporary Resident Visa (TRV) — commonly called a visitor visa — is what most Nigerian passport holders need for tourism, family visits, or short business trips to Canada.

TRV, visitor visa, tourist visa — same thing

These three terms all refer to the same document: the sticker placed in your passport that lets you travel to a Canadian port of entry and request to be let in as a temporary visitor.

Key facts

Validity & stay

  • Usually issued as multiple-entry, valid up to 10 years or your passport expiry
  • Each visit typically allows up to 6 months

Fees

  • $100 CAD per person (or $500 CAD for a family of 5+)
  • Biometrics: $85 CAD per person

Processing time from Nigeria

Processing times change frequently based on application volume — recent published estimates for Nigeria have ranged from roughly 6 to 12 weeks. We check the live IRCC estimate for your visa office before you apply and recommend applying at least 3–4 months ahead of your travel date.

Documents you'll typically need

  • Valid passport
  • Proof of funds for your trip
  • Travel itinerary and purpose of visit
  • Evidence of ties to Nigeria (employment, property, family)
  • Biometrics (fingerprints and photo) at a Visa Application Centre
  • Certified translations for any document not in English or French
