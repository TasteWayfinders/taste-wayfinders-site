UK Tourist Visa (Standard Visitor)

Everything you need to know before applying for a UK Standard Visitor visa — what it covers, what it costs, and the documents that matter most.

Up to 6 monthsPer visit
~3 weeksTypical processing
From £127Standard visa fee

The UK Standard Visitor visa covers tourism, family visits, business trips, and short-term study of up to 6 months. Most Nigerian passport holders need this visa in advance — it isn't available on arrival.

Do you actually need a visa?

Nigerian passport holders need a Standard Visitor visa for the UK — there is no visa-on-arrival or ETA-only route currently available to Nigerian citizens. We confirm your exact requirement before you apply.

What it covers

Permitted activities

  • Tourism and holidays
  • Visiting family or friends
  • Business meetings and conferences
  • Short courses of study (up to 6 months)
  • Medical treatment

Key facts

  • Stay: up to 6 months per visit
  • Fee: from around £127 (6-month visa)
  • Long-term options: 2, 5, or 10-year multiple-entry visas available
  • Processing: typically about 3 weeks

Documents you'll typically need

  • Valid passport with at least one blank page
  • Bank statements or payslips showing financial stability
  • Accommodation details (hotel booking or invitation letter)
  • Travel itinerary (flight plans)
  • Employer letter (for business trips) or proof of ties to Nigeria
Where most applications get refused

Most refusals come down to weak financial evidence or unclear proof that you intend to return to Nigeria — not eligibility itself. We help you present a complete, consistent file before you submit.
