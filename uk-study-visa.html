UK Study Visa

Everything you need to know about the UK Student visa — the points-based system, your CAS, costs, and what to prepare.

70 pointsRequired to qualify
~3 weeksTypical processing
£490–£558Visa fee range

The UK Student visa is for international students aged 16 or over who've been accepted onto a course at a licensed UK institution. It's a points-based route built around your Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies (CAS).

The 70-point system

50 points — Your CAS

A unique reference number issued by your university once you accept an unconditional offer. You cannot apply without one.

10 points — Finances

Proof you can cover tuition and living costs — 28 days of bank statements, held in your name (or a sponsor's) before applying.

10 points — English language

Usually proven through an approved Secure English Language Test (SELT), unless your course provider or nationality exempts you.

Costs to plan for

Visa fee (roughly £490–£558 depending on route) plus the Immigration Health Surcharge — currently around £776 per year of study.

Documents you'll typically need

  • Valid passport and your CAS reference number
  • 28 days of bank statements or a financial sponsor letter
  • English test results (unless exempt)
  • Academic transcripts and certificates
  • ATAS certificate (only for certain sensitive STEM subjects)
  • Parental consent letter, if you're under 18
Dependants

Current UK rules generally only allow you to bring a partner or children if you're on a PhD/research programme or government-sponsored — most taught master's students cannot bring dependants.
