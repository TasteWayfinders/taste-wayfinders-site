Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Who we are

Taste Wayfinders Ltd ("we", "us") is a travel and visa consultation company based in Lagos, Nigeria. You can reach us at info@tastetravels.net.

What information we collect

When you fill in a form on this website, take our visa readiness check, book a consultation, or respond to one of our adverts (for example a Facebook or Instagram lead form), we collect the details you give us. These typically include your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, the country you are interested in, the purpose of your travel, and any message you write. If you become a client, we also handle the documents and information needed for your application.

How we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your visa or travel enquiry, provide consultation and application support, send updates about your application, and improve our services. We may send you information about our services, and you can ask us to stop at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business (for example website hosting, form handling, payment processing, scheduling and database tools) and with partners or authorities where this is needed to process your application and you have asked us to proceed. Our providers process data on our instructions.

Advertising and cookies

This website uses the Meta Pixel to measure how our adverts perform. This may record that you visited a page or submitted a form. You can control ad tracking through your browser settings and your Facebook and Instagram ad preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as needed to deal with your enquiry or application and to meet legal or accounting requirements, and then delete or anonymise it.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information, but no online system is completely secure.

Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or object to how we use it, by emailing info@tastetravels.net. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page.
