Professional processing fees (guide)

If you proceed with us after your consultation, our professional processing fee is separate from the consultation fee and from the visa fee paid to the government. Typical guide rates:

  • UK: ₦300,000
  • Canada: ₦300,000
  • Schengen: ₦375,000 (includes processing, travel insurance and biometrics)

Your final fee is confirmed in writing during your consultation, based on your case. Visa fees are paid to the embassy or visa centre.
