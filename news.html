Latest updates

🇺🇸 USA — Immigrant visa pause affecting Nigeria has been lifted

Nigeria was one of 75 countries whose nationals had immigrant visa issuance paused from January 21, 2026, under a "public benefits reliance" screening policy. Following a federal court ruling, the U.S. Department of State confirmed on August 21, 2026 that the pause is no longer in effect. No previously issued visas were revoked, and tourist (nonimmigrant) visas were never affected by this pause.

Source: U.S. Department of State (travel.state.gov) →

Updated 28 August 2026

🇬🇧 UK — Current visa processing times

For complete applications submitted outside the UK, UK Visas and Immigration's published standard is 90% decided within 3 weeks, 98% within 6 weeks, and 100% within 12 weeks for non-settlement visas (tourist, work, study). Settlement visas: 98.5% within 12 weeks, 100% within 24 weeks. Paid priority service typically returns a decision within 5 working days, and super-priority within 1 working day, where available.

Source: GOV.UK — Visa processing times →

Updated 9 September 2026

🇨🇦 Canada — IRCC processing times for Nigerian applicants

In IRCC's latest weekly update, work permit processing time for Nigerian applicants held steady at approximately 9 weeks, unchanged from the previous update. IRCC's tool refreshes regularly and figures vary by exact application type and visa office, so always check the live tool for your specific case before finalising travel plans.

Source: IRCC official processing-time tool (canada.ca) →

Updated 2 September 2026

🇪🇺 Schengen Area — Current visa requirement for Nigerians

Nigerian passport holders still require a Category C (short-stay) Schengen visa to enter any of the 29 Schengen countries — Nigeria is not on the EU's visa-exempt list. The incoming ETIAS travel authorisation (expected 2027) will not change this, since ETIAS only applies to nationalities that are already visa-exempt. The standard visa fee is €90 for adults, and applications are handled through VFS Global centres in Lagos and Abuja.

Current as of September 2026
