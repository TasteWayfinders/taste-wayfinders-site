About the founder

Opemipo Kuku

CEO and Founder, Taste Wayfinders Ltd

Opemipo founded Taste Wayfinders in May 2026 to give Nigerian travellers and families honest, practical guidance on visas, study, travel and relocation.

Before starting the company, he spent years working in the travel and visa space, including a long stretch as a Head Visa Consultant, overseeing the visa application process for applicants across different destinations. That experience taught him how embassies read an application, and why a well-prepared, consistent file matters more than a rushed one.

He also has a background in financial analysis, which shapes how Taste Wayfinders approaches proof of funds, planning and second-citizenship options, and explains why he insists on clear fees and realistic advice before any application is started.

How we work

We tell you honestly what your options are, quote our fees up front, and never promise an approval. Visa decisions are made by governments, and our job is to help you apply well.
