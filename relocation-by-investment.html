Two routes: citizenship or residence

Citizenship by Investment

You obtain a second passport outright, with no requirement to live in the country. Popular routes include St Kitts and Nevis, Grenada, Antigua and Barbuda, Dominica, St Lucia, Malta, and Türkiye.

Residence by Investment (Golden Visas)

You gain the right to live in the country and travel visa-free across the Schengen area, often as a step toward citizenship later. Popular routes include Portugal, Greece, Malta, Hungary, Italy, Latvia, Cyprus, and Panama.

How the process works

  • 1. Preliminary due diligence — a confidential background check on your passport details, usually completed within one working day.
  • 2. Document preparation — Immigrant Invest's lawyers prepare and certify your personal and financial documents (2–4 weeks).
  • 3. Government due diligence — your application is submitted and reviewed by the relevant government department (2–6 months).
  • 4. Fulfilling the investment — once approved, you complete the required contribution or property purchase (up to 3 months).
  • 5. Passport or residence card issued — usually produced and delivered within 4+ weeks of the investment being completed.
Nigeria-specific note

As Nigeria is not a member of the Hague Apostille Convention, personal documents (birth certificates, marriage certificates, police clearance) prepared for these programmes typically need full consular legalisation — authentication by the Nigerian Ministry of Foreign Affairs, followed by legalisation at the relevant destination country's embassy in Nigeria — rather than a simple apostille. We help you sequence this correctly.
